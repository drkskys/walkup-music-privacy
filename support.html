Walkup Music — Support

Walk-up songs for a youth baseball lineup.

Questions, bugs, or requests: walkupmusicapp3@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you are on — it usually explains the problem straight away.

Getting started

  • Add each player with a name and jersey number.
  • Tap EDIT on a player, then PICK SONG.
  • Choose an audio file from Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive, or pick a song from your Apple Music library.
  • Set START to where the song should begin, as m:ss, and PLAY to how many seconds to play.
  • Drag the ≡ handle to reorder the batting order.

A song will not play

Most often one of these:

  • Apple Music needs an active subscription. This is an Apple requirement and the app cannot work around it. Songs you import as files play without one.
  • The song streams instead of being downloaded. Open the Apple Music app and download it there. The app warns you about this when you assign a song, so you can fix it before a game.
  • The song left your library. If a song is removed from your Apple Music library, the app tells you the queue did not update. Pick the song again to fix it.

Songs go quiet at the field

Ballfields often have poor signal. For anything you truly cannot risk, import the audio file instead of using Apple Music — files stored in the app need no connection at all. Apple Music tracks work offline too, but only once they have been downloaded in the Apple Music app.

Choosing a song from a playlist

Tap PICK SONG then APPLE MUSIC. The picker opens on your playlists; tap one to see its songs. SEARCH ALL searches your whole library by song or artist instead. If a song you just added in Apple Music is missing, tap ↻ REFRESH.

Running more than one team

Open Settings (the gear, top right) and use + ADD TEAM. Each team keeps its own lineup, name and colours. Once you have more than one, tabs appear under the team name for switching between them.

Sharing a lineup

Settings can share the batting order as a text message, and another coach can paste it into IMPORT LINEUP on their own device. Player names and timings transfer; song files do not, so each phone needs its own songs assigned.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app removes your lineups and any audio you imported, so share the lineup to yourself first if you want a copy. See the privacy policy for detail.

Walkup Music is made by one coach, in evenings. Replies may take a few days, but every message gets read.
